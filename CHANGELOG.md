## 1.3.0

- Choose Left, Top, Right, or Bottom independently for nameplate and target frame threat text. Right remains the default.
- Improve Top spacing above nameplate names and the target frame. Bottom clears visible nameplate cast bars and accounts for target frame padding.
- Add a Larger text option to double the threat text size.
- Choose when threat appears: While in a party or raid (default), In dungeons or raids, or Always. Existing saved visibility preferences are preserved.
- Remove the Only show in combat option. Threat text automatically hides when threat data is unavailable.
- Mark default choices in the placement and threat-display menus.

## 1.2.0

- Fix mismatched threat text and colors between nameplates and the target frame.
- Use **Low threat**, **High threat**, and **AGGRO** consistently on nameplates and the target frame.
- Match colors and the optional pulsing warning to these labels. Threat numbers stay the same.

## 1.1.0

- Adds a choice of threat displays on nameplates and the target frame: a percentage, a number without `%`, or text such as "Low threat", "High threat", and "AGGRO".
- Adds colors that change as you get closer to pulling aggro, with an option to use white text instead.
- Adds an optional pulsing warning when you are close to pulling aggro.
- Adds an optional animation that briefly enlarges the threat text when you gain aggro.
- Opens addon settings with `/tthreat` or `/tepiuzthreat`.
- Moves nameplate threat text past crowd-control icons while they are visible, keeping both easy to read.

## 1.0.0

- Show your threat percentage on enemy nameplates and a larger percentage above the target portrait, both drawn with a thick outline.
- Add settings to limit that to combat, nameplates, or the target frame.
