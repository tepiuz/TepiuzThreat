# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Adds a choice of threat displays on nameplates and the target frame: a percentage, a number without `%`, or text such as "Low threat", "High threat", and "AGGRO".
- Adds colors that change as you get closer to pulling aggro, with an option to use white text instead.
- Adds an optional pulsing warning when you are close to pulling aggro.
- Adds an optional animation that briefly enlarges the threat text when you gain aggro.
- Opens addon settings with `/tthreat` or `/tepiuzthreat`.
- Moves nameplate threat text past crowd-control icons while they are visible, keeping both easy to read.

## 1.0.0

- Show your threat percentage on enemy nameplates and a larger percentage above the target portrait, both drawn with a thick outline.
- Add settings to limit that to combat, nameplates, or the target frame.
